
1. Background 
The International Solar Alliance (ISA) is a coalition of 120+ member countries 
working to promote the use of solar energy to address energy needs sustainably. 
ISA’s mission is to mobilize over USD 1 trillion in investments for solar projects by 
2030 and enable energy access in underserved regions. Recognizing the critical 
role of innovation, ISA has initiated several programmes aimed at proliferating 
solar investments and innovations. 
As part of its innovation and startup support initiatives, ISA launched the SolarX 
Startup  Challenge,  a  flagship  program  designed  to  identify,  nurture,  and 
accelerate solar startups across Africa, Asia-Pacific (APAC), Latin America & the 
Caribbean (LAC), and the Middle East & North Africa (MENA). 
With  the  growing  number  of  SolarX  winners  across  multiple regions(50 as of 
now- Africa-20, APAC-20, India-10), ISA recognizes the need for an integrated 
digital platform that can showcase these startups, track their progress, visualize 
regional  data  insights,  and  facilitate  collaboration  with  investors, accelerators, 
and governments. 
ISA therefore seeks to hire a qualified technology firm to develop and maintain a 
Digital  Dashboard  for  SolarX  Winners,  which  will  serve  as  a  real-time, 
public-facing  and  stakeholder-oriented  interface/dashboard  to  showcase  and 
track  the  journey,  performance,  and  support  received  by  each of the winning 
startup. 
2. Objectives 
The primary objective of this assignment is to: 
• Develop a dynamic, scalable, and user-friendly digital dashboard for the 
SolarX Startup Challenge. 
• Showcase profiles of all SolarX-winning startups from different regions. 
• Visualize key data and impact metrics (geography, funding received, sector 
focus, technology maturity, SDG alignment, etc.). 
• Facilitate SolarX application and database- connection with the main 
website. 
• Provide backend access for the ISA team to update and manage startup 
profiles. 
• Ensure compatibility with ISA's web ecosystem and data security 
standards. 
3. Scope of Work 
The selected firm will be responsible for the end-to-end development and 
operationalization of the dashboard, covering the following components: 
A. Requirement Gathering and UI/UX Design 
• Conduct consultations with ISA and relevant stakeholders to understand 
functional and aesthetic requirements. 
• Develop wireframes and UI/UX mock-ups aligned with ISA branding 
guidelines. 
• Finalize dashboard modules and data visualization elements. 
B. Dashboard Development 
• Build a web-based dashboard hosted on ISA’s existing infrastructure or 
recommended secure cloud servers. 
• Create searchable and filterable profiles for each startup, including: 
• Country, startup name, contact details 
• Business summary, technology description, innovation type 
• Accelerator program participation and milestones 
• Funding received, investor connections 
• SDG and climate impact metrics 
• Integrate interactive maps/globe and charts (by region, sector, tech type, 
etc.) 
• Success Stories Showcase 
• Media coverage, Recognition, and other advocacy related events 
• SolarX promotional collaterals/brochures/booklets etc. 
• Call for application: for the SolarX LAC leg 
• Develop multilingual support for regional representation. 
• Implement backend Content Management System (CMS) access for ISA 
staff (I.T. team) to update records. 
C. Integration and Interoperability 
• Integrate dashboard with ISA’s website and future SolarX microsites. 
• Ensure mobile responsiveness and accessibility compliance. 
• Build data import/export capabilities for internal analytics/reporting. 
D. Training, Handover and Maintenance 
• Conduct training sessions for ISA team on using the dashboard. 
• Provide documentation (User Manual, Admin Manual, Troubleshooting 
Guide). 
• Offer 6-months post-launch maintenance and support. 
• Transfer source code and documentation to ISA upon completion. 
  
4. Deliverables 
• Inception  Report: A detailed report outlining the team's understanding of 
the  project,  proposed  methodology,  work  plan,  and  team  deployment 
strategy. 
• Wireframes  &  Mockups: UI/UX design prototypes showcasing the layout 
and user interface of all dashboard modules. 
•  Beta  Version  of  Dashboard: A fully functional version of the dashboard 
with  integrated  features  and  dummy  data  for  testing  and  feedback 
purposes. 
•  Final  Dashboard:  The  live,  production-ready  version  of  the  dashboard 
incorporating feedback from the beta phase and hosted on the designated 
platform. 
•  Training  &  Handover: Training sessions for relevant stakeholders, along 
with  the  submission  of  comprehensive  user  manuals  and  technical 
documentation. 
5. Timeline 
The  project  is  expected to take place over a period of 2 months(May-June 
2025).  The  timeframe may be modified on the basis of mutual consultation 
between the agency and ISA, however, all deliverables should be completed 
within the contract duration. All deliverables should be formatted and submitted 
in ready-to-use/print formats.
